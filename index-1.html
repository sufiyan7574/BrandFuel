The Person Behind BrandFuel

An operator first.
A consultant second.

BrandFuel didn't start with a pitch deck. It started with a D2C fashion brand — EinayaCollection — that Sufiyan Makwani built and has run since 2019, without a co-founder to fall back on and without outside funding to soften the mistakes.

Running a brand teaches you things a marketing dashboard never will — what a stockout actually costs, why a "great" ad can still lose money, and how a slow website quietly erases a week of marketing spend.

That's the experience BrandFuel is built on: customer acquisition, advertising, marketplace operations, website conversion, and the day-to-day economics of a real consumer business. BrandFuel exists to bring that operator mindset to other founders — treating marketing, marketplaces and technology as one connected growth system, not three separate vendors.
